Tab Tidy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Tab Tidy does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

What the extension accesses

Tab Tidy reads the titles and web addresses of your open tabs. It does this only to show them in its popup, to find duplicate tabs, and to save the sessions you choose to create.

What is stored

Your saved sessions and your duplicate-matching preference are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to any third party.

What Tab Tidy does not do

  • It has no servers, user accounts, analytics, or advertising.
  • It does not track your browsing or build any profile of you.
  • It does not load or run code from outside the extension package.

Your control

You can delete any saved session at any time from the Sessions tab. Uninstalling the extension removes all data it has stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
